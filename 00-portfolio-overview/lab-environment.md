## Safety

All testing must remain within an authorized laboratory environment.

Do not upload:

- real passwords
- private keys
- API tokens
- personal information
- real organizational logs
- confidential customer information
- unrestricted malware samples

Sanitize IP addresses, hostnames and usernames when they could identify a real person or organization.
