# Portfolio Objectives

## Primary Objective

Demonstrate practical cybersecurity skills acquired through Cisco CyberOps Associate laboratory activities and organize the evidence against the core competency requirements of TESDA Cyber Threat Monitoring NC I and Cyber Threat Mitigation NC II.

## Learning Outcomes

By completing and documenting the portfolio, the learner should be able to demonstrate evidence of:

### Monitoring

- receiving and interpreting security alerts
- checking detection information
- identifying affected assets
- reviewing security-event information
- validating suspicious activity

### Investigation

- collecting relevant logs and network evidence
- correlating observations
- identifying indicators of compromise
- determining likely attack behavior
- assessing scope and impact
- documenting findings

### Threat Intelligence

- interpreting threat intelligence
- identifying TTPs
- counter-checking IOCs
- determining IOC applicability to the environment
- documenting CTI findings

### Vulnerability Management

- defining a scanning scope
- identifying assets
- performing vulnerability scanning
- analyzing results
- prioritizing findings
- documenting remediation recommendations
- validating remediation

### Mitigation

- recommending or performing appropriate response actions
- blocking or containing indicators where the laboratory environment supports it
- monitoring for recurrence
- documenting mitigation

### Recovery

- identifying a last-known-good state
- documenting recovery or rollback
- validating the recovered system
- conducting post-recovery monitoring

### Reporting

- preparing technical findings
- producing incident reports
- communicating severity and impact
- documenting timelines
- recording lessons learned

## Professional Standard

The portfolio should read like a junior SOC/security analyst's work product rather than a classroom answer sheet.
