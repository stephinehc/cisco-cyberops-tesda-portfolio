# Ethics, Authorization and Laboratory Safety

All activities documented in this portfolio must be performed in an authorized training/laboratory environment.

## Rules

- Do not attack systems without authorization.
- Do not scan public systems unless explicitly authorized.
- Do not publish credentials or secrets.
- Do not upload personal data.
- Do not publish real customer/organizational logs.
- Do not redistribute proprietary course answer keys.
- Keep malware experiments isolated and controlled.
- Preserve evidence responsibly.
- Clearly distinguish simulated incidents from real incidents.

## Philippine Context

Where applicable, consider relevant Philippine cybersecurity, privacy and electronic-transactions requirements, including the Cybercrime Prevention Act and Data Privacy Act.

This repository is educational and does not constitute legal advice.
