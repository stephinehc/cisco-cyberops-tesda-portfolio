# Evidence Quality Standard

## Level 1 — Activity Evidence

Shows that the task was performed.

Example: screenshot of a command or tool window.

## Level 2 — Technical Evidence

Shows what was discovered.

Example: log entry, packet, process, IOC or vulnerability finding.

## Level 3 — Analytical Evidence

Explains what the evidence means and why it matters.

## Level 4 — Response Evidence

Shows the action taken and its result.

## Level 5 — Competency Evidence

Connects the activity, evidence, analysis and outcome to a specific TESDA competency element.

### Portfolio Goal

Aim for Level 4–5 evidence for the core competency claims.
